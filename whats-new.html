What’s new

Every version, newest first.

Carmeo is updated often. This is what changed in each version – on iPhone and Android alike.

  1. Version 1.8.8

    Beta – for testers
    • New: speed on your trips (switch it on in Settings → Privacy → Record speed on routes – off by default): the measured average and top speed, and the route coloured by speed; or coloured electric / petrol.
    • Add another phone with a one-time code or a QR code – no password needed (Settings → Account → Add another phone); two-step sign-in still asks its code.
    • Documents: photos and PDFs already when you add a deadline (up to 5, kept on this phone only) and an optional fee; photos / PDFs (e.g. the invoice) at service log entries too.
    • Refuels: the odometer at every refuel (filled in by itself, can be corrected; asked for a refuel added by hand); the refuel log as CSV / PDF.
    • Connecting a car without the PIN: the data only, no commands, until you add the PIN. A wrong Chery PIN says how many tries are left, and a locked PIN is never tried again.
    • Android: voice commands with Google Assistant / Gemini and shortcuts on the app icon (lock, warm-up, cool-down, climate off, refresh, the car's state).
    • Trip details: the street address of the start and the end, from the phone's own map service. Device log: Carmeo's own automatic actions by name, and where a command came from (widget, Siri, quick button, notification).
    • Battery care: a one-off tip when the battery stays above 95 % for two days. A deeply sleeping Chery gets an SMS wake before a command. Winter and Christmas widgets.
    • Fixes: switching the scheduled charging never sets the car to 0:00 any more; the beta blind buttons are gone (the car does not move the blind on its own); a failed automatic lock shows as unlocked in "While you were away"; Spanish: "Caminar" on the map.

  2. Version 1.8.7

    Latest
    • New: choose your app icon – nine variants, including winter, Christmas and Santa (Settings → Appearance).
    • Automatic lock: a parked car left unlocked for 10 minutes is locked, with a notification; and the car is locked before the scheduled warm-up. Both on by default (Settings → Security, Comfort); places where it should not lock: Settings → Places.
    • "While you were away": a summary at the top of Home when you open the app after 12 hours – trips, charges, locked or not (can be switched off in Settings → Appearance).
    • Places: name a place where you see it – tap the place in a trip, or a long press on the trip map, the Trips page's places or the parked car on Home; a garage / covered parking mark: no frost alert there, and the warm-up follows the garage's temperature.
    • iPhone and Apple Watch: Siri and Shortcuts (lock, warm-up, cool-down, climate off, refresh, the car's state); the Live Activities on the Apple Watch Smart Stack and in CarPlay; Carmeo complications on the watch face; the confirmation from a Control Center button names the command.
    • Omoda / Jaecoo: set the scheduled charging from Carmeo; choose how long the warm-up / cool-down runs (10–30 minutes); rear bench heating on the Jaecoo 8 (beta); clearer messages when the car refuses a setting.
    • Km allowance: the contract term can be set (e.g. 54 months) – the whole term is watched, with a quarterly note at most and a summary 60 days before its end.
    • Prices: a warning for a price that looks mistyped (e.g. 256 instead of 2.56) with a one-tap fix; a place's own price shows under Prices, in the right currency.
    • Fixes: the average consumption in the yearly review and the period summary now comes from what your trips used; no petrol cost for a trip without petrol km; electricity under 1 per kWh counts as free; the Carmeo petrol range of plug-in hybrids; the trunk can always be closed; the blind's own confirmation; the privacy and terms links in your language; What's new in Spanish for every version.

  3. Version 1.8.6

    • Español: the app, the widgets and the notifications in Spanish too.
    • Carmeo 2026 – your year in review on the Trips tab, from December to early January.
    • Trip costs: the fuel at your latest refuel's own price, the electricity at the price of the energy in the battery (nothing after a free charge); the petrol estimated from the petrol km when a full tank's gauge did not move; a refuel on the way is counted.
    • Trips: the nearest town instead of "Map", the odometer at the start and at the end, the month's consumption and cost per 100 km.
    • Charging: "Charge now" for a plugged-in car waiting for its schedule; a smart schedule in the cheap tariff (if you turn it on); Omoda / Jaecoo show the car's charging schedule; country average prices until you set your own.
    • Climate: warm-up and cool-down up to 30 °C; the seat level on its icon; a seat not confirmed yet offers Off first; "My settings back" no longer switches the climate off and runs at once when you unlock with Carmeo; while the car is on, the climate buttons say why they cannot work.
    • Warm-up: "Sent – waiting for the car", a notification when the car did not start it – and when a sleeping car starts it later after all; "The car is asleep – waking it…".
    • Windows and sunroof: the vent button closes open windows; clear questions when closing; words on the sunroof buttons; tilt and the blind as a beta.
    • Places: paste coordinates or a Google / Apple Maps link; service log: the odometer of an earlier day from the trips; a note to add your Home place when there is no weather.
    • Fixes: a new departure time could be lost; "Lock / Unlock" says why the lock state is not known; a parked car's old cabin temperature is no longer shown as new; the charging curve's title on narrow phones.

  4. Version 1.8.5

    • Trips: your own period (from – to) with its numbers and costs; the month's trip log as CSV / PDF with purpose (business / private), a note and who drove (a long press on a trip), the km and cost by driver.
    • The average speed of short trips too – from the part seen moving.
    • Charging: the charger's own kWh (public chargers too) – the cost and the price per kWh follow it; the battery's usable capacity from the charges; tomorrow morning's electric range in the cold; the consumption by outside temperature.
    • Seat heating and ventilation on level 1, 2 or 3 – on the Climate tab and in the warm-up / cool-down; what the parked warm-ups and cool-downs used (kWh, cost).
    • Refuels: a full tank can be marked – the consumption from full tank to full tank.
    • Yearly km allowance (lease, company car): the pace, the expected km, a notice when it goes over.
    • Night quiet hours for the notifications (an open car still sounds); the tyre-check reminder can be switched off; a slow pressure loss is marked on the Tyres tab.
    • Documents: up to 3 photos each, kept on the phone only. Home: the pencil on the car picture edits the favourites – up to 8.
    • Chery: the windows (vent, up, down) and the sunroof from the app – where the car's account allows them (as on Omoda / Jaecoo).
    • Fixes: Refresh no longer switches the climate on (the car showed "On the move"); "My settings back" no longer starts the climate again; the rear window heating shown – grey – where the car allows it only inside; straight, readable number plates on the Tiggo 7 / 8 / 9 pictures.

  5. Version 1.8.3

    • Refuels: the real litres from the pump or the receipt; a refuel can be added by hand.
    • Range: Carmeo's estimate for the petrol side too; on Home you choose right there (Car | Carmeo) which one is the big number.
    • New reminders: a slow pressure loss, winter / summer tyres by the weather (7 °C), hail where the car is parked, a petrol engine not run for long (plug-in hybrid), the expected service date, the factory warranty.
    • Timed warm-up only with the charging cable in; tyre pressure in psi / kPa too.
    • Charging: the time left on DC charges too (when the car sends 0), to your target; a steadier power figure without short "not charging" flashes.
    • Already working since 1.8.2: faster lock / unlock of a sleeping car, "Locked" sooner, more exact trips (no false "charged on the way", no "1 km on petrol" on short electric trips), a faster server.
    • Fixes: switched-off notifications no longer come back after an app start (and a 90 % charge target stays 90 %), the front doors on right-hand drive, fully electric cars (no tank), the whole "Stop at the target" text.

  6. Version 1.8.2

    • Lock, unlock, trunk: the new state shows within seconds, marked "awaiting confirmation" until the car confirms it; "In progress…" no longer jumps back after a minute.
    • Charging loss (Settings → Car → Prices): the costs of the charges and the trips count the energy from the grid; your wallbox's measured kWh can be entered on the charge's page.
    • The month's cost: the fuel the car used, from the tank level – not only the refuels.
    • Tiggo 8: the charging cable on the right side. Widgets: the time left in hours and minutes.
    • Fixes: Google sign-in on iPhone; adding a sign-in with 2FA asks for the code right there; "Not plugged in" says that the charger may have no power; the trip's end position for cars that give it only when asked; the Android update bar without a version number.

  7. Version 1.8.1

    • Favourites: one Lock / Unlock button that follows the car; up to seven buttons in tidy rows.
    • Charging: stop at your target – Carmeo stops the charging there.
    • Charging: a new animation, the target on the main card, and a time left from the charge's own pace when the car's figure is far off.
    • The place of a charge can be set by hand; older charges are named after your places now.
    • Electric range: choose which figure is the main one, or show only the car's.
    • Climate: rear window heating on the top view.
    • Faster warm-up and cool-down: "started" as soon as the climate is on.
    • In December: a little Santa by your car (can be turned off in Appearance).

  8. Version 1.8.0

    • A new look for the whole app: one icon set, calmer cards and clearer rows on every tab, in Settings and on every sheet.
    • Settings: a main page that shows the state of each section, with search; notifications in four groups; departure days with buttons.
    • Lock screen: redesigned charging and warm-up / cool-down cards with the car's picture and the charge target; a new card when the car is left unlocked or a door or window is open, with Lock and Windows up buttons (iPhone Live Activity and Android live notification).
    • Omoda and Jaecoo: pictures of six more models in their own colours (Omoda 5 EV, 7, 9; Jaecoo 5 EV, 7, 8).
    • Shared car: the guest sees their role and only the buttons they may use.
    • Also: sign-in and connecting the car in clear steps; own pickers for time, number and date; confirmations that say what will happen; notifications without emoji.

  9. Version 1.7.6

    • While charging: the energy so far (kWh) and, with a price, about how much it costs – on Charging and Home.
    • While driving "On the move" instead of the lock state; no false "Parked", "Car unlocked" or "Car locked" on the way.
    • The timeline tells when the climate was not started by Carmeo; a hood left open is shown where the car reports it.
    • Android widget: the buttons show that the command is on its way and its result; the lock button shows when the car is already locked.
    • Fixes: service log costs of 100 000 and more; confirmation / Face ID for the first command after opening the app; the remaining charging time after charging; trunk and wake-up commands are checked; the data time no longer jumps back.

  10. Version 1.7.5

    • Vacation mode: no warm-up on the days you choose, a reminder before you are back.
    • Your climate settings come back after a warm-up you started yourself too; a "My settings back" button.
    • The car so far: all km and how many were electric; no false warm-up line on trips; electric km for Omoda / Jaecoo.
    • An electricity price for every saved place; satellite map; the Carmeo range on the widgets.
    • New reminders: "Don't forget to plug in" at home, location recording paused for a week; the car's place after a short re-park.
    • Fixes: bottom sheets no longer under the tab bar, a more solid background, the charging curve's time axis.

  11. Version 1.7.1

    • Trips: Carmeo's own consumption from your trips (7 days, month, all); a trip ends when you lock the car at a stop; charging right after arrival no longer counts as "charged on the way".
    • Plug-in hybrids: the electric range down to your drive mode's limit (Settings → Car).
    • Pause charging where the car has no remote stop (e.g. Tiggo 9): unlock to release the plug, your schedule comes back by itself.
    • Favourite buttons are saved (also when you close the sheet); a clear bar when location recording is paused; sharper, faster maps kept on the phone.
    • Fixes: charging curve, time left on fast chargers, plug icon, UK schedule times and £ prices, warm-up confirmation, cost dialog, fuel in litres, language setting.

  12. Version 1.7.0

    • Charging: alerts at your target %, when it stops early or does not start; the charging curve; a monthly report with CSV / PDF export.
    • Share the car with your family: view only, + climate, or everything; location and trips only if you allow it.
    • New alerts: battery draining while parked, rain with a window open, a check that commands were carried out.
    • Up to three departures a day; after the warm-up your climate settings can come back (beta); climate alerts from 5 minutes.
    • Savings compared with petrol and CO₂, day / night tariff, a service log with export.
    • Favourite buttons, navigate to the car, a map that follows the car, a shareable status picture, a new widget with buttons, the trip in progress in the statistics, manual measurement.
    • Romanian, lei, pounds and miles, right-hand drive; the warm-up before a trip is shown apart.

  13. Version 1.6.5

    • Scheduled charging: set the time and duration, then send them at once; the card shows what was sent until the car takes it.
    • Charges and refuels: enter what they really cost; charges the car slept through are logged too.
    • Trips: better start time and average speed, gaps in the route dashed, place names from your places also on older trips, a clearer dark map.
    • Server status dot on Home; "A new version is available"; places can be edited, one home.
    • Service reminder lead time, first price counts back too, Hungarian car colour names, clearer sign-in steps, many fixes.

    • Comfort timer: a departure time per weekday.
    • Time zone from the car's position (or chosen by you); public holidays by country.
    • Charging: time left in hours and minutes, with the expected end; stop charging (where the car allows it).
    • Scheduled charging read from the car; only the switches your Chery allows remotely; clear message when a command was not done.
    • Low tyre pressure explained, map "Car location" button, update log, fixes (lights on the car picture, large text, deadline dates, settings menu).
