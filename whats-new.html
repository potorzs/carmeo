What’s new

Every version, newest first.

Carmeo is updated often. This is what changed in each version – on iPhone and Android alike.

  1. Version 1.7.0

    Latest
    • Charging: alerts at your target %, when it stops early or does not start; the charging curve; a monthly report with CSV / PDF export.
    • Share the car with your family: view only, + climate, or everything; location and trips only if you allow it.
    • New alerts: battery draining while parked, rain with a window open, a check that commands were carried out.
    • Up to three departures a day; after the warm-up your climate settings can come back (beta); climate alerts from 5 minutes.
    • Savings compared with petrol and CO₂, day / night tariff, a service log with export.
    • Favourite buttons, navigate to the car, a map that follows the car, a shareable status picture, a new widget with buttons, the trip in progress in the statistics, manual measurement.
    • Romanian, lei, pounds and miles, right-hand drive; the warm-up before a trip is shown apart.

  2. Version 1.6.5

    • Scheduled charging: set the time and duration, then send them at once; the card shows what was sent until the car takes it.
    • Charges and refuels: enter what they really cost; charges the car slept through are logged too.
    • Trips: better start time and average speed, gaps in the route dashed, place names from your places also on older trips, a clearer dark map.
    • Server status dot on Home; "A new version is available"; places can be edited, one home.
    • Service reminder lead time, first price counts back too, Hungarian car colour names, clearer sign-in steps, many fixes.

  3. Version 1.6.0

    • Tap a trip: route map, times, electric and petrol km, average speed, estimated electricity and fuel – from Home and from All trips.
    • Fuel and electricity prices: the cost of each trip, charge and refuel, and per month.
    • Comfort timer: a departure time per weekday.
    • Time zone from the car's position (or chosen by you); public holidays by country.
    • Charging: time left in hours and minutes, with the expected end; stop charging (where the car allows it).
    • Scheduled charging read from the car; only the switches your Chery allows remotely; clear message when a command was not done.
    • Low tyre pressure explained, map "Car location" button, update log, fixes (lights on the car picture, large text, deadline dates, settings menu).

  4. Version 1.5.1

    • Your car in its real colour (Tiggo 9, 8, 7) – Settings → Car → Model and colour.
    • Omoda / Jaecoo: only the switches your car has; commands in a row wait for the car.
    • Settings → About → Support: e-mail us with the app version included.
    • iPhone: the charging Live Activity updates on the lock screen.
    • Fixes: phone-number sign-in, false 0 % battery alerts.

  5. Version 1.5.0

    • Safer climate controls and a "Turn the climate off" button; choose what Warm-up and Cool-down switch on.
    • Security: confirm every command, Face ID before unlocking, two-step sign-in, signed-in devices, device log, Sign in with Google.
    • Notifications per phone; settings easier to find, with search.
    • Whole-day timeline, readable charts, fixes.

  6. Version 1.4.1

    • A bar on Home when commands are paused because of the vehicle PIN – with a Reconnect button.
    • Faster feedback: the new state shows within seconds; a sleeping Chery is woken first.
    • Accurate charging log: recharging while driving and a false 0 % are not charging.
    • Clearer sign-in messages; fixes on Android, tiles and pop-ups.

  7. Version 1.4.0

    • Chery: connect with your Chery account password – e-mail or phone number. The password is never stored.
    • Clearer PIN field; after a wrong PIN you can try again right away.
    • Settings → About shows the app version.
    • Tiggo 7 PHEV (UK) recognised.

  8. Version 1.3.1

    • Expired e-mail links: a clear message and a button for a new confirmation e-mail.
    • Closing the car connection screen no longer causes an error.
    • Tiggo 7: charged energy calculated with its 18.4 kWh battery.

  9. Version 1.3.0

    • Hungarian language: the app, the widgets and the notifications.
    • Cabin temperature on the Climate page; outside temperature from the weather.
    • Set Home and your other places on the map.
    • More stable live connection; clearer charging power.

  10. Version 1.2.0

    • Charging power in kW – Charging page, Home, widgets, Live Activity.
    • Save your Carmeo password in the Passwords app or Google Password Manager.
    • Omoda and Jaecoo: windows and sunroof.
    • A short "What's new" after every update.

  11. Version 1.1.0

    • Omoda and Jaecoo support: remote lock, unlock and climate.
    • Commands show whether the car confirmed them – or why they failed.
    • Battery and tank size per car model, adjustable in Settings → Car.
    • Error reports to our own EU server, no third-party tracking.
