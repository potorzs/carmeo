Security

Built for something as valuable as your car.

Two-step sign-in, a list of every signed-in phone, a lock on the sensitive commands – and clear rules about what Carmeo stores.

Become a tester

Two-step sign-in and your devices.

Protect your account with an authenticator app. See every phone that is signed in, sign a lost one out from afar, and get an alert on every new sign-in.

  • Device log – sign-ins and commands by device, kept for 90 days.
  • No IP address or location – is stored about your devices.

A second look before the car unlocks.

Ask for a confirmation before every remote command, or for Face ID or a fingerprint before the sensitive ones: unlock, trunk, windows and sunroof.

Share the car, not your password.

Invite up to five people. They use their own Carmeo account, and you decide what each may do: just look, look and use the climate, or everything.

  • Location is off by default – it is shared only if you switch it on.
  • Revoke at any time – access ends the moment you remove someone.

What Carmeo stores – and what it never does.

Your car password is used once to sign in to the car cloud and is never stored. The sign-in keys and your vehicle PIN are kept with AES-256 encryption, on servers in the EU. No ads, no analytics.

  • Pause location recording – trips are then logged without places.
  • Export or delete – download everything Carmeo stores, or delete your account, from the app.
