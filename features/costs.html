Costs and consumption

What does driving really cost you?

Enter your own fuel and electricity prices once. Carmeo turns every trip, charge and refuel into money – and shows what you saved by driving electric.

Become a tester

Your prices, your currency.

Fuel per litre, electricity at home and electricity elsewhere – each with its own price. When a price changes, the new one counts from that day on.

Today, this week, this month.

Distance, electric kilometres and electric share side by side – with the month’s electricity, fuel and service costs underneath.

  • Saved with electricity – what the electric kilometres would have cost on petrol.
  • CO₂ avoided – an estimate, compared with driving the same distance on petrol.

Cost per trip and per charge.

Every trip shows the energy and fuel it used and what that cost. Every charge shows its price – estimated from your tariff, or the real amount if you enter it.
