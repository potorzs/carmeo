All features

Trips and routes

Every trip, logged automatically.

No start button and no logbook to fill in. Carmeo notices each trip and keeps the distance, the electric share and the cost.

Become a tester

Your day as a timeline.

Today’s trips at a glance – and a timeline of the day with the rest: charges, locking and unlocking, the climate switching on, in the order they happened.

Where you have been.

The trips map shows the places you visited in the last 7 or 30 days, numbered by how often you were there. Name your regular places and they appear by name everywhere. Name a place right where you see it – with a long press. Mark it as a garage: no frost alert there, and the warm-up follows the garage’s temperature.

Each trip in detail.

Distance, time and average speed, how much was driven on electricity and how much on petrol, the energy and fuel used – and what the trip cost with your own prices. Next to your trips, the nearest town’s name – worked out on Carmeo’s own server, without an outside service.

Location recording can be paused at any time. Trips are then logged without places.
