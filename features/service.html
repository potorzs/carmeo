Tyres, service, documents

Nothing about your car slips through.

Tyre pressures, the next service, the vignette, the MOT and the insurance – Carmeo keeps the dates and reminds you in time.

Become a tester

Tyre pressure, wheel by wheel.

Pressure and temperature for each tyre, with a warning below the limits you set. A regular reminder tells you when it is time to check them at the pump.

The next service – by kilometres or by date.

Carmeo counts down to the next service, whichever comes first. The service log keeps every service, tyre change and repair with its cost, and exports it as CSV or PDF when you sell the car.

Documents and deadlines.

Motorway vignette, insurance, MOT: add the expiry date once, see the days left and get a reminder before it runs out.
