Alerts

A nudge when it matters. Silence when it doesn’t.

Carmeo watches the car so you don’t have to – and you decide, phone by phone, which alerts ring, which arrive quietly and which stay off.

Become a tester

The things you would want to know.

Each alert says what happened and where, and the list in the app keeps the latest ones.

  • Left open – the car left unlocked, a door or a window open – after the waiting time you choose.
  • Charging – started, finished, charged to your target, stopped early, or plugged in but not charging.
  • Weather – frost tonight, the car parked in the heat, rain coming while something is open.
  • The car itself – low battery or fuel, low tyre pressure, the battery draining while parked.
  • Security – your car is moving – not you? – and every new sign-in to your account.

Off, Quiet or Normal – for every alert.

Every phone has its own settings. Quiet means no sound and no banner: the alert simply waits in the notification list.

Reminders, too.

A service coming up, the regular tyre pressure check, a vignette, MOT or insurance about to expire – Carmeo reminds you in time.
