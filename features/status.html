Status at a glance

Everything about your car, on one screen.

Battery, fuel and range, locks, doors and windows, tyres and where the car is parked – updated around the clock, with your own car in its real colour.

Become a tester

Battery, fuel and range at a glance.

The home screen opens with what you check most: the charge level and electric range, the fuel level and how far the tank still takes you. Carmeo’s own range estimate covers the petrol side too, and a Car | Carmeo switch on the home screen sets which one is the big number. While you were away: open the app after 12 hours and a summary waits at the top – trips, charges and whether the car was locked.

  • Your car in its own colour – pick the model and paint once, and the picture follows.
  • Locked or not – a green lock on the picture, and a warning if something is left open.
  • How fresh it is – the time of the last update is always on screen.

Where it is parked – and the way back.

Carmeo shows the car on the map with the name of the place and since when it has been standing there. One tap opens the route to the car in your maps app.

  • Your own place names – Home, Office, Gym – name your regular places once.
  • Recording can be paused – switch location recording off whenever you like.

Doors, windows and tyres, wheel by wheel.

The Vehicle screen shows every door, every window and the trunk from above. The Tyres screen shows the pressure and temperature of each wheel.

Always fresh, without keeping the car awake.

Carmeo asks for new data about every 2 minutes while you drive or charge, and every 10 minutes when the car is parked. After a command it refreshes right away.
