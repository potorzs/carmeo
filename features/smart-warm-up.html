Smart warm-up

A warm car every morning. A cool one in summer.

Tell Carmeo when you leave. It checks the forecast and starts the climate just in time – only on the days when it is needed.

Become a tester

Set your departures once.

Choose the departure time for each weekday – even two a day, for the way to work and the way home. Public holidays are skipped.

The weather decides.

Below 10 °C Carmeo warms the cabin up, above 22 °C it cools it down, and in between it leaves the car alone. Both limits are yours to set.

  • A heads-up 20 minutes before – not driving today? Tap Skip today.
  • Starts 15 minutes before you leave – with the temperature, seat and steering wheel heating and defrost you chose.
  • Frost and heat warnings – a note before a frosty night, and one when the car stands in the heat.

The countdown follows you.

The home screen shows the minutes to departure and what the car is doing. On iPhone the countdown also runs as a Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.
