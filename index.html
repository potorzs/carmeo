How it works

Up and running in three minutes.

  1. Install Carmeo

    Create a free Carmeo account with e-mail, Apple or Google.

  2. Connect your car

    Sign in with your Chery or OMODA | JAECOO account. Tip: share the car with a second account, so the official app stays signed in too.

  3. That’s it

    Carmeo keeps an eye on your car around the clock – every 2 minutes while you drive or charge, every 10 when parked.

Privacy and security

Your car, your data.

Carmeo needs your car account to talk to your car. Here is exactly what happens to it – and to everything else the app stores.

Read the Privacy Policy

  • Your car password is never stored

    It is used once to sign in to the car cloud, then deleted.

  • Keys and PIN are encrypted

    The sign-in keys for your car and your vehicle PIN are stored with AES-256 encryption.

  • Stored in the EU

    Your data lives on servers in Ireland and Germany.

  • No ads, no analytics

    No profiling and no selling of data. Your data runs the app – nothing else.

  • Pause location history

    Switch off location recording whenever you like – trips are then logged without places.

  • Export or delete anytime

    Download everything Carmeo stores, or delete your account and all logs, right from the app.

FAQ

Questions?

Write to support@getcarmeo.app – a real person answers.

Which cars work with Carmeo?

Cars that work with the official Chery app or the OMODA | JAECOO app in Europe. Tiggo 7, Tiggo 8 and Tiggo 9 are tested daily by our users.

Why does Carmeo need my car account?

Your car only talks to the manufacturer’s online service. Carmeo uses that same service, through your own account – there is no other way to reach the car. Your password is used once to sign in and is never stored; the sign-in keys are kept encrypted.

Will the official app keep working?

The car clouds allow one signed-in app per account. Share your car with a second account in the official app and connect that one to Carmeo – both keep working.

Can my car account get into trouble?

Carmeo is unofficial, and the manufacturers’ terms restrict access by other apps – so they could restrict an account that uses Carmeo. That is why we recommend connecting a second account the car is shared with, not your main one. Details are in the Terms of Use.

What if the manufacturer changes its service?

Then a feature can stop working until Carmeo is updated. Carmeo depends on the same online services as the official app, and the manufacturers can change them at any time.

How fresh is the data?

About every 2 minutes while driving or charging and every 10 minutes when parked. After a command Carmeo refreshes right away.

Does Carmeo keep the car awake or drain the battery?

No. Carmeo reads what the car has already reported to the manufacturer’s online service, and asking for it does not wake the car. Commands do wake it, just as in the official app – and Carmeo can alert you if the battery drains while parked.

Which languages and units does it support?

English, Hungarian, Romanian and Spanish. Costs in euro, forint, lei or pounds, distances in kilometres or miles, and a right-hand-drive view.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Invite up to five people with their own Carmeo account and decide what each may do: just look, look and use the climate, or everything. Your location is shared only if you switch it on.

Does it cost anything?

No. Carmeo is free to use.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account – everything linked to the account is deleted at once. You can export your data first. Without the app, see Delete your account.

Who makes Carmeo?

An independent hobby developer in Budapest who drives a Tiggo 9 himself. Carmeo is not connected to the car manufacturers, and it carries no ads.
